Konstantin Tsagkalidis

Originally from Hamburg, I earned my Bachelor’s in Communication and Media Science in Leipzig. Alongside my studies, I gained professional experience in B2B/B2C social media, competitive reporting, and video editing.

Seeking a more hands-on creative path, I moved to Salzburg to specialize in Film (MultiMediaArt). Today, I balance my own projects with constant experience on student sets.

Selected Works

GOODBYE STRANGER

Short Film Editor Colorist 1st AC

A short film centered on conversational storytelling where I served as on-set 1st AC, editor, and colorist. I focused on creating unobtrusive, motivated editing choices, ultimately delivering a cinema DCP export in DaVinci Resolve. The film is currently making its festival circuit.

SET ME FREE (WIP)

Music Video DP Editor

Set Me Free translates a first same-sex love into emotionally distinct visual worlds, each designed as a parallel inner space rather than a narrative location. The visual approach draws on haptics as a core principle, using texture, movement, and material presence to make an often unspoken emotional experience tangible and accessible.

KORD FREITAG

Documentary Live Visuals TouchDesigner MIDI

Visual identity development for an up-and-coming Salzburg punk band, with a primary focus on live visuals. I used TouchDesigner and MIDI controllers to create dynamic performance visuals that capture the band's energy. I'm also following the band with a camera for a documentary to be released in the future, as we continue developing their overall aesthetic direction.

OSCAR WINNING TEARS COVER (WIP)

Music Video DP Lighting Design

Cinematography and lighting design for a music video cover, working from minimal direction to develop a visual concept that stays minimal yet engaging. I completed the project on a tight turnaround with release planned for YouTube.

MINDSET IS ...

Animation Stop Motion TouchDesigner

A hand-drawn stop motion animation exploring the gap between the rhetoric of "mindset" and the messier realities it tends to gloss over. Each frame was drawn by hand and the finished animation was then run through TouchDesigner.
